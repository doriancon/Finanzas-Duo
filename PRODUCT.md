# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Stack
React, TypeScript and Vite, following the user's preferred stack. Local persistence for demo data.

## Users
Dorian and Dani use one shared everyday finance view to see how much they spent, what remains in the month and where spending goes.

## Product Purpose
Manage shared everyday spending across currencies, savings and a weekly Pasanaco fund while tracking a separate lingerie venture.

## Operating Context
Mobile-first daily use, with a desktop layout. Accounts may contain both shared and business movements. Each currency remains distinct.

## Capabilities and Constraints
One monthly everyday spending limit per selected currency. Business expenses affect account balances but do not count toward this limit. Business sales may enter general accounts but are tagged to avoid double counting in income goals. Categories are editable. This phase is frontend demonstration with local persistence, no bank connection, real authentication or multi-device synchronization.

## Brand Commitments
Duo Finance; soft premium fintech, light surfaces, colorful rounded finance cards, accessible concise Spanish copy. User-provided images are visual references, not UI assets to copy.

## Evidence on Hand
The supplied product brief and functional correction. All financial figures in the app are illustrative.

## Product Principles
- Make today's and this month's spending immediately understandable.
- Keep currencies and business versus everyday movements explicit.
- Make routine recording fast and forgiving.
- Support two people without splitting the shared household budget.
